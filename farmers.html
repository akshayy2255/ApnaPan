How to become a supplier

Four steps, one page of paper, no paperwork in a language you cannot read

The whole agreement fits on one page in Kannada and you keep a signed copy. If anything in it is violated, you can call the founder's number printed at the bottom.

STEP 1

Tell us about your farm

Send the form below or a WhatsApp message with your village, district, crops and expected quantity. Two minutes is enough.

STEP 2

A visit from our field team

We come to your field within 10 days, look at the crop, take a soil sample and give you a written indicative rate.

STEP 3

Sign the season agreement

A one-page agreement in Kannada: quantity, rate, delivery date, payment date, quality expectations. You keep a copy.

STEP 4

Harvest, weigh and get paid

We collect at your village, weigh in front of you and transfer payment to your account within 7 working days.

Help & contact

What is the minimum quantity you buy?
For chilli, coriander, turmeric, groundnut and dal we buy from 300 kg upwards. For mango midi and amla we buy from 200 kg. If you are a very small grower, we can group you with three or four neighbours in the same village and buy together.
Do you pay above the mandi rate always?
Our agreement rate is set in advance and is on average 22% above the local mandi rate for the same grade — but the honest answer is that in some weeks the mandi goes higher than us. If the market rate rises by more than 10% before harvest, we revise your rate upward. We have never revised one downward.
What quality do you look for?
Clean, sorted produce with proper drying. For chilli, under 10% moisture; for coriander, under 9%; no mould, no stones. We give you a moisture meter and teach you to use it — that alone raises your price by a grade.
Are you certified organic?
We buy both organic and conventional produce and label each honestly. Our Jaivik Bharat certification for a small organic line is in process. We do not pay a premium for a certificate you do not have — bring us clean, properly dried produce and we pay for that.
Do you buy from outside Karnataka?
For now our sourcing is 100% Karnataka — six districts. As we add processing capacity we will look at neighbouring states for mustard oil and select spices.
