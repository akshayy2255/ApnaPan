How it works

From a farmer's field to your kitchen in five careful steps

Five steps, each with a check that a person signs their name to. This is what happens between a farmer's field and the jar in your kitchen.

1 Sourcing

Bought at the farm gate, never at a mandi

Our field coordinator visits the farm before harvest, tests moisture on the spot with a handheld meter and agrees the grade-wise rate with the farmer in writing. On collection day we weigh at the village on a scale the farmer can read, print a slip, and take the whole lot — not just the best bags.

22%above mandi rate, 2025
11 cropsbought direct
2 Processing

Slow-roasted, stone-ground, small batch

Spices are shade-dried for two days, then roasted in 12 kg batches in a drum roaster. Whole spices are added in a fixed order — coriander and cumin first, dals next, chilli last — because each reaches its right point at a different time. Grinding happens on a slow stone mill that keeps the oils in the powder instead of heating them out.

3 Quality control

Every batch tested, and one woman who can stop the line

Our in-house lab checks moisture, salt, pH, water activity and microbial load on every lot. A sensory panel of three tastes each batch against a retained reference sample. Our quality head, Nasreen Taj, has the authority to reject a batch outright and the founder cannot overrule her. Rejected lots are never blended into good ones.

7 testsper batch
100%lots traceable
2 batchesrejected in 2026 so far
4 Packaging

Sealed the same week, with names on the label

Glass jars for pickles and masalas that need to stay warm and aromatic; food-grade laminated pouches for ready mixes. Every pack carries a batch code, the packing date, the FSSAI licence number, the name of the woman who packed it and the farmer who grew the main ingredient. Nitrogen flushing is used for mixes to push shelf life without preservatives.

5 Distribution

Out of the door in 24 hours

Orders received before 4pm are dispatched the same day by our own team, from our unit to doorstep couriers, retail partners in Karnataka and Maharashtra, and gifting orders for corporates. Direct-to-home customers get a WhatsApp message with the tracking link the moment the pack leaves the building.
