The story in this jar

Baby mango pickle is a June project. The mangoes are picked while the stone is still soft — usually the last week of May to mid-June — and must go into salt within a day of plucking. We split each midi at the tip, pack it with hand-pounded mustard, Byadagi chilli, fenugreek and rock salt in mustard oil, and then it is simply sun and time: fourteen days on our terrace, stirred every morning, no acetic acid and no preservatives.

How to use it

Serve 2–3 midis with curd rice, dal rice or chapati. The oil at the top is part of the pickle — spoon it over, do not drain it.

Storage: Always use a dry spoon. Keep the mangoes under the oil layer. Do not refrigerate before opening. · Shelf life: 18 months; improves for the first 6 · Spice level: Medium-hot

Shipping & returns

Where do you deliver and how much does it cost?
We deliver across India. Free shipping on orders above ₹599; a flat ₹49 below that. Bengaluru orders usually arrive in 1–2 days, the rest of India in 3–6 days.
How long do the products stay fresh?
Masalas and mixes: 6–9 months from packing, printed on the pack. Pickles: 15–18 months. We roast and grind in small batches every Tuesday and Friday, so the pack you receive was usually made within the last three weeks.
What is your returns policy?
If anything arrives damaged, leaking or is not what you ordered, tell us within 7 days on WhatsApp and we replace it or refund it — no photographs of forms required if it is clearly our mistake. Taste is a fair reason too: if a masala genuinely does not work for you, we will refund your first jar.
